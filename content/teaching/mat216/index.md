An introduction to linear algebra and Fourier analysis, emphasizing geometric intuition and connections between ideas. Materials below are organized by semester.

## Course outline

**Before the midterm**

- Row operations and why they preserve the solution set
- Gaussian elimination and Gauss–Jordan elimination
- General solutions of linear systems and column combinations
- Vector spaces, subspaces, linear combinations, independence, span, basis, and dimension
- Linear transformations and the four fundamental subspaces

**After the midterm**

- Eigenvalues, eigenvectors, and diagonalization
- Inner products and the Gram–Schmidt process
- Periodic functions and Fourier series, including half-range sine and cosine series
- Complex Fourier series, Fourier transforms, and sine and cosine transforms
