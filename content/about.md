I am Emon Hossain, a mathematics lecturer at BRAC University in Dhaka, Bangladesh. My interests include differential geometry, algebraic geometry, theoretical physics, and computational mathematics. I also enjoy programming and exploring how deep learning connects with mathematical research.

## Mathematics and research

My undergraduate final-year project explored deep learning in [computational fluid dynamics](https://github.com/emonhossainraihan/Koopman-and-FEM). My Master's thesis focused on Calabi–Yau manifolds. Working across computation and pure mathematics has shaped the questions I like to explore.

An earlier interest in cryptography and cybersecurity led to a [paper on chaotic-map cryptosystems](/papers/paper1/) with Awnon Bhowmik and Mahmudul Hasan. Read my [CV](/cv.pdf) for my academic background.

## A path through programming

My interest in programming began at Saint Joseph Higher Secondary College, when my friend [Shaswata Das](https://github.com/shaswata56) encouraged me to learn C. Building a calculator together sparked an interest that later grew to include Python, JavaScript, and web development.

During my undergraduate years, I explored data science and spent time learning frontend and backend development. I also worked for a small IT company and built projects including [COVID-19 monitoring](https://github.com/emonhossainraihan/covid19), a [shopping-cart application](https://github.com/emonhossainraihan/add-to-cart), and a [microservice experiment](https://github.com/emonhossainraihan/mimic-micro-service). My [web development notes](https://github.com/emonhossainraihan/web_dev_notes) record some of that learning.

I learned web scraping with [Md. Abu Taher](https://github.com/entrptaher), and began exploring cryptography with [Awnon Bhowmik](https://github.com/awnonbhowmik). I have also helped maintain the [Eulerian](https://www.facebook.com/groups/eulerian) and [Undergraduate Math](https://www.facebook.com/groups/undergraduatemath) communities to encourage learning in technology and mathematics.
